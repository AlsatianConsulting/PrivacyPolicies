# TrapHouse Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 7, 2026

TrapHouse is developed by Alsatian Consulting.

This Privacy Policy explains what information TrapHouse processes, how that information is used, and your choices.

## Summary

- TrapHouse processes all data entirely on your device.
- TrapHouse does not require an account.
- TrapHouse does not operate a backend service and does not transmit data to Alsatian Consulting or any third party.
- TrapHouse does not sell personal information.

## Information Processed by the App

TrapHouse may process the following categories of information on your device:

- Photos you capture using the device camera within the app
- Photos you select from your gallery as a reference image
- Temporary session files stored in app-private storage while a session is active

## Why the App Processes This Information

The app uses this information to provide core functionality:

- Display a live camera preview with a semi-transparent reference image overlay for alignment
- Capture a comparison photo aligned to your reference
- Save before/after photo pairs to your gallery on request

## Data Storage

- Temporary session files are stored in app-private internal storage (`files/session/`) and are cleared when you start a new session or reinstall the app.
- Photos you choose to save are written to device gallery storage under `Pictures/TrapHouse`.
- The app sets `allowBackup="false"`, so session data is not included in Android backups.

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

TrapHouse does not connect to any network and does not share data with Alsatian Consulting or any third-party service. No analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs are included in the app.

## Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

- **Camera** — required to display a live preview and capture photos.
- **Read media / external storage** — requested when you select a reference photo from your gallery.
- **Write external storage** (Android 9 and below only) — required to save photos to the gallery on older devices.

You can deny or revoke permissions in Android settings. Revoking camera access will prevent the app from functioning.

## Retention and Deletion

You control your data on your device.

- Session files are automatically cleared when you start a new session.
- Saved photos can be deleted with your gallery or file manager app.
- Uninstalling the app removes all app-private session data.

## Security

TrapHouse uses standard Android platform security controls. All releases are signed with a release keystore. No data leaves your device.

## Children

TrapHouse is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated over time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
